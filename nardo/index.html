The layer

Not a feature pile. One key, Caps Lock, and everything composes as layers on the same stacked surface: bar, cards, strip, sheet. A card tears off into a window. The mini browser grows into the full browser on Enter. Glance, card, window, app, without switching.

Held items

Hold the noun, type the verb. Find a file, lift it above the bar, then type at it: under 100mb, mail susan, done. The thing stays held across every step. Modules add verbs, so there is nothing to open and no app to switch to.

Terminal

libghostty under a bare grid. Shells live in a small daemon, so they survive rebuilds and quits. Prompt marks turn output into blocks you can jump, copy and drag out. A card taps you when a long command finishes out of sight.

Surface

An infinite canvas on its own engine: nodes and props, reversible ops, branching history, incremental SQLite saves. Boards, ink, notebooks, PDFs. Everything draws from the document, so undo is a jump, not a replay.
